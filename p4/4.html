Vision

To provide quality education, encourage innovation and research, and develop talented individuals who can contribute positively to society and the nation.

Mission

  • Provide quality education with practical knowledge.
  • Encourage creativity, innovation, and new ideas.
  • Promote research and technological development.
  • Develop responsible and ethical professionals.
  • Create strong connections with industries and organizations.

Program Specific Outcomes (PSOs)

  1. Use technical knowledge to solve real-life problems.
  2. Develop creative and useful solutions for industries.
  3. Use modern software and technologies effectively.
  4. Improve communication, teamwork, and leadership skills.
  5. Follow professional ethics and continue learning new skills.
